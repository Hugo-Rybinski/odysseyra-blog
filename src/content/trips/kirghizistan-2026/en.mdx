Fifteen days in Kyrgyzstan, from Bishkek to the high plateaus of Son-Kul and Köl-Suu, then around Issyk-Kul before heading back through the Chuy valley. A little over 2,100 km by car, a good share of it on dirt tracks. Each region has its map, its stops and the practical notes worth knowing before you go.

<Region id="bichkek">

Morning arrival at Manas after a connection in Istanbul. The day goes to the city centre: Ala-Too Square, the Soviet architecture walk and the main museums.

<Tip kind="money">
The transfer from Manas airport costs about 1,800 KGS for two in a taxi booked through the hotel. Have som in cash from the moment you land.
</Tip>

</Region>

<Region id="son-koul">

Pick up the car in Bishkek and head for Kochkor for the regional museum and the bazaar. From there, the climb to Son-Kul goes over the Kalmak-Ashuu pass.

<Photo id="DSC01117" />

<Tip kind="road" at="kalmak-ashuu">
The Kalmak-Ashuu road is a mountain track, described as off-road for much of its 120 km. Allow 2 h 30 from Kochkor and don't set off after mid-afternoon.
</Tip>

A full day at the lake: walks around the camp, an optional horse ride, and the herding life that sets the pace on the plateau.

<Tip kind="sleep" at="son-koul-camp">
The yurt camps on the south shore have no signal and no constant power. Nights drop below freezing in late September: bring a proper sleeping bag.
</Tip>

<Tip kind="border" at="naryn-permit">
The Köl-Suu area is a border zone: pick up the permit in Naryn before going on. The office is in a building with a red lattice front. Without a permit, the checkpoints turn you back.
</Tip>

<Tip kind="road" at="kol-suu-track">
After Naryn, about 130 km and 4 h of mountain track to the Kychtto jailoo. Fill up in Naryn: there is nothing after.
</Tip>

<Photo id="DSC01520" />

The next day, an out-and-back hike to the lake, sunk in its gorge. Once at the water, follow the accessible shore for the viewpoints.

Back along the track to At-Bashy for the Kochoy Korgon fortress and its small museum, then on to the Tash Rabat valley. The stone caravanserai is built around a central domed hall. The following day, a long climb to the Chatyr-Kul pass at 4,025 m for the view over the lake.

</Region>

<Region id="issyk-koul-sud">

A long driving day: At-Bashy, Naryn, Kochkor, Ottuk then Bokonbaevo, with a stop at the Manjyly-Ata springs before the night in Ton.

<Tip kind="road" at="skazka">
The turn-off for Skazka canyon is about 4 km before Tosor, and the last 2 to 3 km are on a track. Stay on the visible paths: the formations erode quickly.
</Tip>

On through the Tamga Tash petroglyphs and the yurt workshop in Barskoön, then a detour into the Jeti-Ögüz valley and the Seven Bulls before Karakol.

In Karakol, the Dungan mosque, Holy Trinity Cathedral and the history museum. In the afternoon, up through Ak-Suu to Altyn Arashan for the hot springs.

<Tip kind="road" at="altyn-arashan">
The track from Ak-Suu to Altyn Arashan is off-road all the way: allow 2 h one way for 31 km in a suitable vehicle.
</Tip>

</Region>

<Region id="jyrgalan">

From Karakol to Jyrgalan, a former mining village, for a loop hike to Turnaluu-Köl lake. The Kök-Bel waterfall is an optional detour.

The next day, the Chon Ak-Suu valley from Grigorievka, the Cholpon-Ata petroglyphs, then across through Balykchy to Konorchek canyon and its red rock formations, before the night in Tokmok.

</Region>

<Region id="tchou">

<Tip kind="info" at="tokmok-market">
The Tokmok livestock market runs on Sundays and starts well before sunrise. Get there early: it is all over by noon.
</Tip>

On to Kegeti for the hike to Köl-Tör lake, a steep trail up to the turquoise water, then the Kegeti waterfall.

<Tip kind="road" at="kol-tor">
Past the Kichi Kegeti valley, the road to the trailhead includes off-road sections.
</Tip>

Last driving day: Burana tower, the Suyab and Navekat ruins, the Ata-Beyit memorial and then Ala-Archa national park, before returning the car in Bishkek.

<Tip kind="info">
The flight home leaves very early: plan to be at Manas 2 h 30 before departure, a 35-minute drive from the centre.
</Tip>

</Region>

<Regrets>

- Leave Kochkor earlier for Son-Kul: the [Kalmak-Ashuu track](#pt-kalmak-ashuu) is longer than it looks.
- Stay two nights at [Köl-Suu](#pt-kol-suu-camp) instead of one, to do the hike without the pressure of the drive back.
- Split the [Tash Rabat](#pt-tash-rabat) to [Ton](#pt-ton) day in two: 350 km in one day is too much.

</Regrets>
