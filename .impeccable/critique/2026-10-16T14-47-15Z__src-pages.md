## Verdict
- The page chrome is very much on-world; the map tiles are off-world (a dark steel negative).
- Coherence is about 75%.
- Detector:
  - 10 font sizes off the scale.
  - `.uid` contrast at 2.47:1 (real, chosen on purpose).
  - The stripes finding is a false positive (the drafting grid).
- Inventory:
  - Leaflet zoom keeps 2px radius, a #ccc divider and Lucida Console (specificity beats SheetMap.astro:108).
  - Georgia "i".
  - Blurred shadows on 39 pins.
  - Weights 800 and 500 off the scale.
  - 5 uppercase label styles, only 2 of them tokens.

## Collisions
1. A positive page around a negative map.
2. Graph paper around the text, but no grid on the map.
3. Engineering title block versus cartouche: needs a revision table.
4. Live slippy map versus "scanned": the texture is invisible.

## Priority issues
1. **[P1] Map tiles contradict the world.**
   - Negative look, inconsistent between zooms, seams at fractional zoom.
   - Fix: two-ink SVG ramp filter, seam fix.
   - Commands: colorize, then polish.
2. **[P1] References.**
   - 57 tab stops at 2.47:1; they clip the scale on mobile maps.
   - Fix: local part only, tabindex -1, ink-3, hidden on maps under 640px, explained in the legend.
   - Command: distill.
3. **[P2] Small rule breaks.**
   - Glued "·" ×4, about-page discs, "→" glyph, Georgia "i", Leaflet × and corners, pin shadows, mono counts, three box weights.
   - Command: polish.
4. **[P2] Mobile sticky chrome of 140px.**
   - Fix: a single row of about 40px.
   - Command: adapt.
5. **[P3] No legend on the region maps.**
   - Fix: a micro legend in the bottom strip.
   - Command: clarify.
