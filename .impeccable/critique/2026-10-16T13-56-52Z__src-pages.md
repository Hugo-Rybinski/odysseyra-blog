## Priority issues
1. **[P1] Box references.** 73 `a.uid` on the trip page, 11px at 2.46:1, each a tab stop (298 focusables).
   - Fix: show on hover or focus-within of the box and in print, `tabindex=-1`, or ink-3 if they stay visible.
   - Commands: distill, then audit.
2. **[P1] Text lines of 110–125 characters.** Panels are 960px wide at 17px (global.css:155-160).
   - Fix: limit the text to about 68ch inside the 960 panel.
   - Command: typeset.
3. **[P1] Mobile.** The sticky head is 120px plus a 20px progress bar. Targets: Leaflet zoom 30×30, uid about 30px, home author links 37px, a.go 36×44, progress segments 50×19.
   - Fix: a one-row head of about 44px once scrolled, a progress bar at least 32px tall, Leaflet controls at 44px under coarse pointers.
   - Command: adapt.
4. **[P1] Missing content looks finished.** About page prompts, the "Exemple :" note, 6 photos without captions, EXIF absent on all 15 photos.
   - Fix: a "Relevé en cours" stamp, a build-time warning for missing EXIF or captions, author notes kept out of production.
   - Command: harden.
5. **[P2] The trip has no ending and no freshness date.**
   - Fix: a "Bilan" cartouche with key warnings and regrets, the export repeated, the date checked, a link back to the index, the coming-soon sheets.
   - Command: shape.
